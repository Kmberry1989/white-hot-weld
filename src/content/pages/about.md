Anchored in love for one another, secure in the Lord, immovable... merged... welded... married metal art.

I've always had a fascination and desire to create art using metal as a media. Years ago I tried a short stint of creating some pieces of art with copper and copper wire but could never find the right way to bring it all together. (I had no good way of joining it)

My husband John is a welder by trade and when he brought home a shiny new tig welder in 2015 my aspirations for creating metal art started becoming a reality.

John taught me the fine art of welding and with much practice on my part. Guidance, love and patience on John's part came the beginning of:

White Hot Weld ~married metal art~

God's timing is perfect.

## Artists
- Sheryl | Sculptural Direction | Shapes movement, silhouette, and rhythm so each piece feels alive before the final polish.
- John | Fabrication Lead | Engineers joints, balance, and material behavior to make ambitious forms hold over time.

## Studio Values
- Fewer pieces, better made.
- Honest materials and visible process.
- Work that settles into a space and keeps speaking.
