# White Hot Weld
## Website Vision & Build Plan

**Brand essence**
White Hot Weld is married metal art—fire, partnership, and craft fused into objects that feel both industrial and intimate. The site should feel *hot but precise*: sparks against clean steel, romance without sentimentality, craft without clutter.

Tagline anchor:
**“Married Metal Art… Began with a Spark.”**

This plan stays deliberately modular so the site can grow from a beautiful brochure into a full‑blown digital foundry without tearing itself apart later.

---

## 1. Core Visual Direction (non‑negotiables)

Think: dark steel, glowing heat, controlled motion.

- **Palette**: charcoal / gunmetal base, warm whites, ember orange, occasional electric blue for contrast.
- **Texture**: subtle steel grain, brushed metal gradients, heat bloom glows (very restrained).
- **Typography**: one strong industrial serif or condensed grotesk for headers; clean modern sans for body. No novelty fonts—this is serious fire.
- **Motion**: slow sparks, gentle parallax, hover glows like hot metal cooling. Never frantic.

This direction works whether the site is 5 pages or 500 products.

---

## 2. Phase 1 — The “Gorgeous Presence” Site (Low Lift, High Impact)

This is the minimum viable beauty.

**Pages**
- **Home** – cinematic hero, clear tagline, 3–4 signature works, emotional hook
- **About** – Sheryl + John, marriage + metal, origin story
- **Gallery** – curated projects (not a dump)
- **Contact** – commissions, inquiries, social proof

**Homepage flow**
1. Full‑bleed hero image or video (sparks in slow motion)
2. Tagline + one‑sentence philosophy
3. Featured works (3–6 max)
4. “Meet the Artists” teaser
5. Call to action: commissions / gallery

This version could live happily on Wix Studio, Squarespace, or a lightweight custom build.

---

## 3. Phase 2 — Storytelling Upgrade (Emotional Gravity)

If they lean into *story* rather than scale, this is where the site shines.

**Additions**
- **Process Page** – from raw steel → finished art
- **Timeline / Origin Story** – marriage, first weld, first sale
- **Behind the Mask** – studio photos, tools, failures, experiments

**Interactive touches**
- Scroll‑triggered reveals like sparks igniting
- Before/after sliders (raw steel vs finished art)
- Subtle audio option (torch hiss / forge ambience)

This turns the site into a digital gallery experience.

---

## 4. Phase 3 — Commission‑Ready Functionality (Business Mode)

If sales and inquiries increase, the site should support it gracefully.

**Features**
- Commission request form with guided questions
- Size / material / indoor‑outdoor selectors
- Timeline expectations (handmade honesty)
- Reference image uploads

**Optional upgrades**
- Pricing tiers or “starting at” ranges
- Waitlist / booking calendar
- Automated response with next steps

No e‑commerce pressure yet—this stays personal.

---

## 5. Phase 4 — Shop / Catalog Direction (Only If It Makes Sense)

Two possible paths here:

### A. Curated Drops
- Limited pieces
- Release announcements
- High drama, low inventory

### B. Permanent Collection
- Wall art
- Small sculptural objects
- Outdoor installations

**Technical notes**
- Shopify or Snipcart backend
- Heavy imagery, minimal UI chrome
- Each piece gets a story, not just a price

---

## 6. Phase 5 — Experimental / Signature Ideas (Optional but Spicy)

Only if they want to stand out nationally.

- **Heat Map Gallery** – color overlays showing weld intensity
- **Interactive Spark Cursor** – tiny sparks on hover
- **Metal Journal** – thoughts, sketches, half‑finished ideas
- **AR Preview** – see artwork on a wall (future‑leaning)

None of this is required. All of it is memorable.

---

## 7. Platform Strategy (Flexible)

- **Fast start**: Wix Studio or Squarespace
- **Scalable + custom**: Next.js / React + CMS
- **Hybrid**: Start hosted, migrate later without redesign

Design first. Tech follows.

---

## 8. What We’d Need From Them (Eventually)

Not all at once. No overwhelm.

- 10–20 strong photos (even phone photos can work initially)
- Short origin story (rough draft is fine)
- List of services they *like* doing vs avoid
- One sentence describing their ideal client

The site can evolve as clarity emerges.

---

## Final Thought

This site should feel like standing near a weld—warm, dangerous, beautiful, and deliberate. It’s not just a portfolio. It’s proof that two people can build something enduring together, one spark at a time.

The plan is built to grow without losing soul.
